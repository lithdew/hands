# Independent review protocol

This is a creative-quality evaluation of the actual Hands output. A completed workflow and valid HTML are prerequisites, not a sufficient verdict. Record the real run UUID, model trace, screenshot paths and observed interactions. Do not edit generated files while reviewing.

## Required observations

Inspect both pages at 1440×900 and 390×844. Look at the first viewport, the active workbench, and a lower-page work section. Open the project from home and return. Change the workbench task; step or replay; pause/reset or choose another stage. Confirm that the labels, visible graphic and output preview agree. Try the primary control with keyboard focus. Check reduced motion. Separate observed behavior from source-only conclusions.

## Scoring anchors

Score each dimension from 1 to 5 with a sentence tied to a concrete screenshot or behavior.

- **Identity and art direction:** 1 is a stock theme or arbitrary styling; 3 is coherent but familiar; 5 is a recognizable paper/ink/cobalt identity with a strong visual signature.
- **Typography and hierarchy:** 1 is unedited default type or collisions; 3 has clear hierarchy but predictable scale; 5 combines expressive display type, disciplined readable copy and precise small annotations.
- **Composition and spacing:** 1 is generic repeated cards or accidental gaps; 3 is balanced but safe; 5 uses asymmetric alignment, varied rhythm and whitespace to guide the eye across a complete page.
- **Workbench graphic:** 1 is decorative, static or incoherent; 3 explains the idea clearly; 5 is an original instrument-like visual whose changing geometry and labels make the handoff understandable.
- **Interaction quality:** 1 is dead/fake controls or confusing state; 3 is functional and predictable; 5 makes task selection, sequencing and feedback feel crisp, discoverable and rewarding without excessive motion.
- **Mobile craft:** 1 is a shrunken desktop or broken layout; 3 is responsive and readable; 5 is deliberately recomposed with confident scale, compact navigation and a clear usable workbench.
- **Content and restraint:** 1 is vague sales language or invented biography; 3 is accurate and useful; 5 is concise, specific, warm and clearly about Ishaan's current work, with provenance kept out of the visitor's way.

Creative acceptance requires no dimension below 3 and a median of at least 4. Do not raise scores merely because no overflow occurred or because generation was expensive. A repeated template, fake primary interaction, materially false claim, inaccessible primary control or major responsive defect blocks acceptance regardless of the median.

If a repair is needed, identify at most three high-impact observed changes. Preserve strong work. Feed those concrete observations back into the Hands workflow; the coordinator must not author replacement final HTML/CSS/JS. Re-inspect the changed views and behaviors after the actual repair. Record a pass only when the result materially improves on the earlier technically correct but generic site.
